House Plants Database
===============
![A schema design for my house plants database.](./house_plants/house_plants_schema.pdf)  
   
OVERVIEW
--------
A database I created to help me from accidentally killing my partners houseplants.

---

#### Why Did I Build This?
My partner loves buying plants for the flat. 
The problem is when she goes back home to Greece. 
Suddenly I am responsible for their fate.
This is my first attempt at designing and building a database from scratch, and a valuable source so I don't get it in the neck when she's back.


#### What I Have Learned...So far:
First of all, what a mistake building this thing. Now I have no excuses.
However, from a database design perspective I have learnt to think about how to break down this idea into tables with their relationships. 
As well as work out what type of data will be handy to have, and what details I can probably scrap. 
I got to dabble with dbdiagram.io to create the schema, and learning that it turns it into a handy postgreSQL download.
Having the pdf is also a very handy reference guide for when adding data into my database.
